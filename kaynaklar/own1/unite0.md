Adverbs of frequency
Adverbs of frequency
always usually often sometimes hardly ever never

e Weuse adverbs of frequency to say how often
something happens.
e We put them before the main verb, but after the verb
to be.
| often message my friends.
There are always music festivals in summer.
e Often, sometimes and usually can also come at the
beginning of the sentence.
Sometimes, my family and | listen to music.
Expressions of frequency
| every day / week / weekend / year
| once / twice / three times | a day / a week / a month/a year |
e We also use expressions of frequency to say how
often something happens.
e We usually place them after the verb phrase, but they
can also come at the beginning or end of a sentence.
| see my friends twice a week.
Every weekend, they give a concert.
e Wecan use an expression of frequency and an adverb
of frequency in the same sentence.
! usually go on holiday once a year.
Present simple and present continuous
e Weuse the present simple to talk about facts, habits
and routines.
My sister likes rock music. | use my phone every day.
e Weuse adverbs of frequency (always, often, etc.)
with the present simple and at the moment and now
with the present continuous.
My dad often plays computer games with me.
My teacher is walking into the classroom now.
e We also use the present simple to talk about future
planned events if a future time expression is used or
understood.
My mum starts work at 10 am on Friday.
Hannah can’t come shopping with me this afternoon.
