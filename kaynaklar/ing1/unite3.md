Louis Braille was born in 1809 in France. He became blind because of an
accident when he was 3 years old. It was difficult to learn by listening to his
teachers, so he left his school and attended an institution for blind people.
In 1821, with the help of Charles Barbier, an old soldier, Louis developed
a system of dots and dashes to read. He used his fingers to slide over a
pattern. That pattern had letters. A lot of people use this system nowadays.
L. Braille died in 1852.
Alexander Graham Bell was born on March 1847 in Scotland. His father
always wanted him to carry on the family business, but Alexander was
interested in inventing new things. When he was 16, he began to work
with his father. In 1870, his father decided to move to Canada. Some
time later, Alexander moved to Boston and went on working on his new
inventions there. He is best known for his invention of the telephone.

Murad II. Mehmed II became Sultan twice, first when he was 12 and again
when he was 19. He really wanted to capture Constantinople. Although
everybody thought it was impossible, Mehmed conquered the city in 1453.
He ruled the Ottoman Empire for 30 years. He was a brave soldier and an
excellent statesman. He died on 3rd May, 1481.
